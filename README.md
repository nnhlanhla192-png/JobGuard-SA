# JobGuard-SA


## Overview
JobGuard SA is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform that helps South African job seekers identify fake job advertisements, fraudulent recruiters, phishing links, and recruitment scams before applying for jobs or sharing personal information.

## Problem Statement
Many South African job seekers fall victim to fake recruitment opportunities. Scammers use fraudulent job advertisements, fake companies, phishing links, and registration fees to steal money and personal information.

Job seekers need a simple and reliable way to identify suspicious opportunities before becoming victims.

## Our Solution
JobGuard SA uses artificial intelligence and cybersecurity tools to analyse job advertisements, recruitment messages, and website links to identify potential scams.

The system provides a risk assessment and explains why a job opportunity may be suspicious.

## Key Features
- **AI Scam Detection:** Analyses job advertisements and recruitment messages.
- **Phishing Detection:** Identifies suspicious and potentially malicious links.
- **Company Verification:** Checks available company information.
- **Recruiter Verification:** Identifies suspicious recruiter details.
- **Risk Assessment:** Classifies opportunities as low, medium, or high risk.
- **Scam Reporting:** Allows users to report suspicious job opportunities.

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** React, HTML, CSS
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **AI:** Large Language Models (LLMs)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Cloud:** AWS
- **Security:** Threat Intelligence APIs

## How It Works
1. User submits a job advertisement, message, or link.
2. AI analyses the information for scam indicators.
3. The system checks available security and company information.
4. JobGuard SA generates a risk assessment.
5. User receives an explanation and safety recommendations.

## Target Users
- Job seekers
- Students and graduates
- Unemployed individuals
- Recruitment agencies

## Project Goal
To protect South African job seekers from recruitment fraud, phishing attacks, financial scams, and identity theft through accessible AI-powered cybersecurity technology.
