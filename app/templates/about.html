ABOUT JOBGUARD SA

Safer Job Hunting Starts Here.

JobGuard SA is a job-seeker safety project designed to help South Africans recognise suspicious job offers, learn about recruitment scams and make safer decisions when applying for work.

We want job seekers to pause and verify before sharing personal information or paying money to someone claiming to be a recruiter.

Our goal

Make job-scam awareness easier to access and encourage people to look out for one another.

Our safety principles

  • Protect personal information.
  • Encourage users to verify recruitment claims.
  • Clearly distinguish examples from verified reports.
  • Never treat an automated warning as absolute proof of fraud.
